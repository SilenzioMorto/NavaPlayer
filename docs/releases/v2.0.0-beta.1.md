# Nava Player 2.0.0 Beta 1 — "Lights On"

The biggest update yet: Nava is now a full Windows app with a brand-new GPU engine, AI lyrics that run on your own PC, and a complete sound lab.

> ⚠️ **Beta.** Things may still change before the full release. Please report anything odd in [Issues](../../issues).

## ⬇ Download
**`NavaPlayer-Setup-2.0.0-beta.1.exe`** — Windows 10/11 64-bit · see the [installation guide](https://github.com/SilenzioMorto/NavaPlayer/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md).

## 🆕 What's new

### A native Windows app
- New installer with an English/Persian language picker, an optional **desktop shortcut** and registration as a **music player** (*Open with*, *Default apps*, double-click to play).
- New uninstaller that asks why you're leaving and can clean up your data.
- **System tray** support with a "quit or keep playing?" prompt, plus start-with-Windows.
- Animated **loading screen**, **welcome** and **goodbye** messages, and a first-run **setup + guided tour**.

### New GPU visual engine
- Rebuilt from scratch in **WebGL2**; the old version's stutter is gone.
- **16 visuals**, new: **Black hole** and **Prism**.
- Bloom, echo trails, warp zoom, chromatic beat effects and particle systems.
- **40 hand-tuned presets**, saving your own presets, and auto-switching.

### AI lyrics
- Synced lyrics generated **locally** with Whisper (large-v3 / turbo) via whisper.cpp — **Persian, English, Russian**.
- **CUDA acceleration** on NVIDIA GPUs (a 4-minute song takes about 15 seconds).
- Much better **Persian**: automatic large-v3 model, voice detection, and text clean-up.
- Saved as **`.lrc`** files in the folder you choose; the next song is prepared in advance.
- **16 lyric animations**, including **3D Extrude, 3D Fly-in, 3D Cube and 3D Orbit**.

### Sound lab
- **10-band equalizer** with a draggable live curve and 20 presets.
- **Pitch** (±12 semitones), speed, vinyl mode, **reverb** presets, **bass boost**, **8D audio**, stereo width, balance, mono, loudness normalization, fades and a **sleep timer**.
- **Volume booster up to 300%** with a limiter, a warning, and a 10-minute loudness reminder.

### Library
- Scans folders or all drives, with album, artist, folder, favorite and queue views, play counts and resume.
- **Skips tracks shorter than your limit** (default 30 s) unless you pick them.
- Skips tiny system and game sound files.

### Links
- Play **Spotify, SoundCloud and YouTube** links through their official players, with the visuals following the music.
- Play **direct audio links and internet radio** through the full audio engine.

### Personalisation
- **English & Persian** interface (full right-to-left).
- **Six themes**, including the new **Sakura**, plus a **custom theme builder**.
- **Rebindable hotkeys** that only work while Nava is focused, so they never fire inside games.

## 🐞 Known issues
- The installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows a warning on first run.
- AI lyrics are less accurate on very busy mixes and very fast rap.
- Spotify plays 30-second previews until you log in inside the embedded player.
