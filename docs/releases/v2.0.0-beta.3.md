# Nava Player 2.0.0 Beta 3 — "Drop"

Bigger visuals, harder-hitting bass, and lyrics that land on the exact word.

## ⬇ Download
**`NavaPlayer-Setup-2.0.0-beta.3.exe`** — Windows 10/11 64-bit · [installation guide](https://github.com/SilenzioMorto/NavaPlayer/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md)

Already installed? Run the new installer over it. Your settings, library and fonts are kept.

## 🆕 New
- **12 new visuals** (28 in total): Pulsar, Hive, Vortex, Terrain, Radar, Lava lamp, Mandala, Plasma, DNA helix, Code rain, Cells, Fractal.
- **Every kick hits the scene:** a punchy zoom-in, a light flash (theme colour or white), and a directional camera shake with a touch of rotation.
- **Particles on the beat:** the floating particles swell, rush towards you and release a soft glow on every bass hit. Size, reaction strength and glow are adjustable.
- **Lifeless until the music hits:** keep the scene dark and colourless until the bass kicks in or the singer starts, then watch light and colour flood back. Choose how dark, how sensitive, how fast, and whether it wakes on bass, vocals or both.
- **Scene light controls:** overall brightness, glow threshold and a highlight limit, so the picture never glares when nothing is happening.
- **Glass panels:** turn the controls, library, settings and dialogs into frosted glass with the visuals flowing behind them. Blur and transparency are adjustable, and there's a quick toggle in the top bar.

## ✨ Improved
- **Lyrics timing:** each word now gets its own timestamp from the model's attention alignment (DTW), mapped back onto the real song timeline. Karaoke and word-by-word styles land on the word as it is sung. Older lyrics are regenerated automatically the next time you play the song.
- **Persian lyrics** keep that word timing even after the Persian text clean-up.
- **Calmer defaults:** bright visuals such as Horizon no longer glare when the song is quiet.

## 🐞 Fixed
- The lyrics badge sometimes showed "CPU" even when the graphics card was doing the work.
