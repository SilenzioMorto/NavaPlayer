# Nava Player 2.0.0 Beta 2 — "Typeface"

A focused update about how your lyrics look.

## ⬇ Download
**`NavaPlayer-Setup-2.0.0-beta.2.exe`** — Windows 10/11 64-bit · [installation guide](https://github.com/SilenzioMorto/NavaPlayer/blob/main/docs/INSTALL.md)

Already on Beta 1? Run the new installer over it. Your settings, library and lyrics are kept.

## 🆕 New
- **Lyrics fonts:** pick one font for Persian lyrics and another for English and Russian lyrics, set the weight from Light to Black, and see a live preview.
- **Bring your own fonts:** import `.ttf`, `.otf`, `.woff` or `.woff2` files in *Sound & scene → Lyrics → Import font files*, or just drop them onto the Nava window. Imported fonts stay on your PC.

## ✨ Improved
- Welcome and goodbye messages now end cleanly, without a full stop.

## 🐞 Fixed
- Karaoke, Word by word, Flying words and Bouncing word styles kept putting every word on its own line. Lines now stay on one row and wrap naturally.
